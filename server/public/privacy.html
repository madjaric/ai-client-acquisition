01Information We Collect

Account information

When you sign up, we collect your email address and optionally your name and company. This is used to manage your subscription and communicate service updates.

Usage data

We collect anonymized usage data — pages visited, features used, error logs — to improve the Service. This data is not linked to your identity.

Lead data

Lead data (business names, addresses, contact info) that you import is stored in your own SQLite database instance. We do not access, read, or copy this data.

02How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the Service
  • To send transactional emails (receipts, password resets)
  • To respond to support requests
  • To detect and prevent abuse

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it to data brokers, or share it with third parties except as required by law.

03Third-Party Services

LeadFlow AI integrates with the following services:

  • SerpAPI — for Google Maps search. Your keyword queries are sent to SerpAPI. See SerpAPI Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini AI — for scoring and email generation. Lead data is sent to Google's API. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Gmail SMTP — for email delivery. Emails you send pass through Google's mail servers.

04Data Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. Upon account deletion, data is purged within 30 days. Email logs and generated messages are stored indefinitely unless you delete them manually or request deletion.

05Security

We implement industry-standard security measures. API keys and passwords are stored as environment variables, never in source code or the database. For more details, see our Security page.

06Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@leadflowai.com. We respond within 30 days.

07Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced via email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
