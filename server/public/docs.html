Overview

LeadFlow AI is an AI-powered lead generation and outreach platform built for agencies and B2B teams. The platform has six core modules:

  • Dashboard — a real-time overview of your pipeline, top-scored leads, and campaign activity
  • Leads — manage your prospect pipeline with status tracking
  • AI Scoring — automatically rank leads by estimated value and conversion potential
  • Campaigns — organize outreach sequences by type (email, LinkedIn, cold call)
  • Email Generator / Outreach Generator — AI tools to write personalized cold emails, subject lines, and LinkedIn DMs
  • Send Email — SMTP-based email sending with delivery logging

Quick Start 5 MIN

  1. 1
    Open the App
    Go to leadflowai-2nl0.onrender.com and sign in. No install required.
  2. 2
    Add Your First Lead
    Click + Add Lead in the Leads section. Enter a business name, industry, and location. Optionally add a website URL and notes.
  3. 3
    Score the Lead with AI
    Navigate to AI Scoring → click ⚡ Score a Lead, select your lead, and run the AI scorer. You'll receive a score (0–100), tier (A–D), estimated value, and confidence rating.
  4. 4
    Generate a Cold Email
    Go to ⚡ Generator. Select your lead, choose a tone (Auto, Consultative, Aggressive, Casual, etc.), and click Generate All Formats. You'll get a full email, subject line, LinkedIn DM, and personalization notes.
  5. 5
    Send the Email
    Head to ◉ Send Email. Verify your SMTP, select the generated message, enter the recipient's email, and hit Send. All sent emails appear in your audit log.

Leads

The Leads module is your central prospect pipeline. Each lead has a status that you can update as you progress through your outreach:

  • new — freshly added, not yet contacted
  • contacted — outreach has been sent
  • qualified — lead has expressed interest or fits your ICP
  • converted — became a customer or signed a contract
  • lost — not interested or unresponsive

Adding Leads

Click + Add Lead and fill in: Business Name (required), Industry (required), Location (required), Website (optional), Notes (optional). Notes are passed to the AI generator for richer personalization.

Filtering & Search

Use the status filter dropdown to view leads by stage. Refreshing the view syncs with the latest data from the server.

AI Scoring

The AI Scoring module evaluates each lead and assigns a score from 0 to 100, a tier (A, B, C, or D), an estimated deal value, and a confidence percentage.

ℹ

Scoring uses a combination of the lead's industry, location, website presence, and any notes you've provided. The more context you add, the more accurate the score.

Score Tiers

  • Tier A (75–100) — High-value targets, prioritize immediately
  • Tier B (50–74) — Good fit, worth nurturing
  • Tier C (25–49) — Possible fit, lower priority
  • Tier D (0–24) — Poor fit, deprioritize

Email Generator GPT-4o

The Outreach Generator produces three outputs simultaneously from a single lead and tone selection:

  • 📧 Full Email — subject line + complete email body
  • 💬 Short DM — concise LinkedIn message (under 300 characters)
  • 🎯 Personalization Notes — talking points and hooks specific to the lead

Tones

  • Auto — AI picks tone based on lead score (high score = consultative; low score = soft)
  • Consultative — advisory, positions you as an expert
  • Soft — low-pressure, relationship-focused
  • Aggressive — direct, urgency-driven
  • Casual — friendly and informal

Generation History

Every generated message is saved to your history with the lead name, subject, score, tone, and date. Click any row to restore the content.

Send Email (SMTP)

Connect your own email account to send outreach directly from LeadFlow AI. This works with Gmail SMTP, custom SMTP servers, or any provider that supports SMTP.

Setup

  1. 1
    Verify SMTP
    Click ◎ Verify SMTP and enter your host, port, username, and password. For Gmail, use smtp.gmail.com:587 with an App Password.
  2. 2
    Compose
    Select a generated message from the dropdown, or compose manually. Set recipient email, optional reply-to, and link to a lead for tracking.
  3. 3
    Send & Monitor
    Hit Send. The Email Log shows delivery status, timestamp, and source for every email sent.

API Reference AGENCY

The LeadFlow AI REST API is available on Agency plans. Base URL:

Base URL
https://leadflowai-2nl0.onrender.com/api/v1

Authentication

Include your API key in the Authorization header on every request:

HTTP Header
Authorization: Bearer lf_live_xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx

Leads API

MethodEndpointDescription
GET/leadsList all leads (supports ?status= filter)
POST/leadsCreate a new lead
GET/leads/:idGet a single lead by ID
POST/leads/:idUpdate lead fields or status
DEL/leads/:idDelete a lead permanently
JavaScript — Create Lead
const res = await fetch('https://leadflowai-2nl0.onrender.com/api/v1/leads', {
  method: 'POST',

});
const lead = await res.json();

Billing & Plans

Plan details, upgrade/downgrade, and billing history are managed from your account settings inside the app. For refund requests, see our Refund Policy. For plan comparison, see the Pricing page.

CSV Export

Pro and Agency plans can export their full leads database as a CSV file. Navigate to the Leads section and click the Export button in the toolbar. The export includes all lead fields and their current AI scores.

White Label AGENCY

Agency plan users can remove LeadFlow AI branding from client-facing exports and reports. White-label settings are available in Account Settings → Branding. You can set your agency name and logo, which will appear on all exported PDF/CSV reports.
