Platform features

Search Google Maps by business type and city. Get real local businesses with ratings, reviews, phone, and website info instantly.

FAQ

Common questions

What data source does Lead Discovery use?
LeadFlow uses SerpAPI's Google Maps integration to pull real local business data — ratings, reviews, phone numbers, and website presence. When SerpAPI is configured, all data is live.
Which AI model powers the scoring and email generation?
Scoring uses Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite for speed. Email and outreach generation uses Gemini 2.5 Flash, which produces higher-quality personalized copy.
Can I send emails directly from LeadFlow?
Yes — LeadFlow integrates with Gmail SMTP. Connect your Gmail and app password, and you can send personalized emails directly from the dashboard with full delivery logging.
Is there a free plan? What are the limits?
The free plan includes 10 leads/month and 20 AI-generated emails. No credit card required. Upgrade to Pro ($49/mo) for 500 leads, 2,000 emails, and CSV export.
Does it comply with CAN-SPAM and GDPR?
All outreach campaigns include automatic unsubscribe management, required legal footers, and rate-limiting. Data sources include only publicly available business information.
Can I export my leads to a CSV?
Yes — Pro and Agency plans include CSV export from the Discovery page and Leads pipeline. You can also export "leads without a website" as a targeted subset for outreach campaigns.
