💡 TL;DR: If you're not happy within your first 7 days of a paid plan, email us at billing@leadflowai.com and we'll refund you in full. No receipts, no interrogation.

01Free Plan

The Free plan has no associated charges and is therefore not subject to refunds. You may cancel or switch plans at any time with no financial impact.

02Monthly Subscriptions

Within 7 Days of First Charge

If you upgrade to a paid plan and request a refund within 7 calendar days of your first charge, you will receive a full refund of that charge. This is a one-time courtesy for new paid subscribers.

After 7 Days

Monthly subscription charges after the initial 7-day window are generally non-refundable. However, we handle exceptional circumstances on a case-by-case basis. Contact us at billing@leadflowai.com to discuss your situation.

Cancellation

When you cancel a monthly subscription, your access continues until the end of the current billing period. No partial-month refunds are issued.

03Annual Subscriptions

Annual plans are billed upfront for 12 months at a 20% discount over monthly pricing.

04Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refunds under any circumstances:

  • Add-on services or one-time purchases
  • Charges for periods of service already used
  • Accounts terminated due to Terms of Service violations
  • Charges that occurred more than 180 days prior to the refund request

⚠ Chargebacks initiated through your bank or card provider without first contacting us may result in permanent account suspension. Please reach out to us first—we're happy to resolve billing issues directly.

05Downtime & Service Credits

If LeadFlow AI experiences downtime that breaches our SLA (99.5% for Pro, 99.9% for Agency), you are eligible for service credits, not cash refunds. Credits are calculated as a percentage of your monthly fee proportional to excess downtime and applied to your next billing period automatically.

06How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email billing@leadflowai.com from the email address associated with your account and include:

  • Your account email and company name
  • The charge date and amount
  • Reason for the refund request (optional, but helpful)

We aim to respond within 2 business days. Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days and returned to the original payment method.
