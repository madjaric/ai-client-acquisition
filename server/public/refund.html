TL;DR: If you're not happy within your first 7 days of a paid plan, email us at billing@leadflowai.com and we'll refund you in full. No receipts, no interrogation.

01Free Plan

The Free plan has no associated charges and is therefore not subject to refunds. You may cancel or switch plans at any time with no financial impact.

02Monthly Subscriptions

Within 7 days of first charge

If you upgrade to a paid plan and request a refund within 7 calendar days of your first charge, you will receive a full refund. This is a one-time courtesy for new paid subscribers.

After 7 days

Monthly subscription charges after the initial 7-day window are generally non-refundable. However, we handle exceptional circumstances on a case-by-case basis. Contact billing@leadflowai.com.

Cancellation

When you cancel, your access continues until the end of the current billing period. No partial-month refunds are issued.

03Annual Subscriptions

04Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refunds:

  • Add-on services or one-time purchases
  • Charges for periods of service already used
  • Accounts terminated due to Terms of Service violations
  • Charges that occurred more than 180 days prior to the refund request
Note: Chargebacks initiated through your bank without first contacting us may result in permanent account suspension. Please reach out first — we're happy to resolve billing issues directly.

05Downtime Credits

If LeadFlow AI experiences downtime that breaches our SLA (99.5% for Pro, 99.9% for Agency), you are eligible for service credits — not cash refunds. Credits are proportional to excess downtime and applied to your next billing period automatically.

06How to Request

Email billing@leadflowai.com from your account email and include:

  • Your account email and company name
  • The charge date and amount
  • Reason for the refund request (optional, but helpful)

We aim to respond within 2 business days. Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to the original payment method.
