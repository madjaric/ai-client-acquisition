FAQ

Common questions

What counts as a "lead" in LeadFlow AI?
A lead is a unique local business record discovered by our AI crawler, enriched with contact info, reviews, social presence, and ad spend signals. Each unique business counts as one lead against your monthly quota. Leads reset on your billing date.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan anytime?
Yes—upgrades take effect immediately and you'll be prorated for the remainder of your billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the next billing date. You'll never lose access to your data when downgrading.
What AI models power the email generation?
LeadFlow AI uses a fine-tuned ensemble of GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 Sonnet, trained specifically on cold email copywriting data. Our models understand industry-specific angles and automatically personalize based on the lead's reviews, website, and social activity.
Is there a refund policy?
We offer a full refund within 7 days of your first paid charge if you're not satisfied—no questions asked. After 7 days, refunds are handled case-by-case. Annual plan refunds are prorated for unused months.
Can I white-label LeadFlow AI for my clients?
White-label is available on the Agency plan. You can remove all LeadFlow branding from exported reports and the client-facing dashboard. Custom domain support for the portal is available as an add-on.
Does LeadFlow AI comply with CAN-SPAM and GDPR?
Yes. All outreach campaigns include automatic unsubscribe management, required legal footers, and rate-limiting to avoid spam signals. Our data sources only include publicly available business information. We also offer a data deletion API for GDPR compliance.
